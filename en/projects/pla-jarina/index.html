SUSTAINABLE MATERIALS · RESEARCH

PLA composites reinforced with jarina seed powder.

Experimental study of Amazonian natural-filler composites, including specimen manufacturing and mechanical testing.

Universidade Federal do Amazonas · PIBIC/PAIC2024–2025
01 / The challenge

The challenge

Investigate whether jarina seed powder can serve as a natural filler for PLA while addressing preparation, blending and molding challenges.

02 / My work and methods

My work and methods

I worked on seed preparation and grinding, polymer blending and specimen manufacturing. The workflow involved a SolidWorks-designed mold, CAM preparation and machining, followed by ASTM D638 (Type IV) tensile tests.

03 / Results and takeaways

Results and takeaways

Three PLA/plasticizer formulations with 0%, 3% and 5% jarina were investigated. The study found difficulties with matrix–filler adhesion, brittle samples and thermal processing. Results were exploratory and presented orally at CONIC 2025.
