COMPUTER VISION · TEAM PROJECT

Robot-assisted 3D reconstruction of a shoe.

Point-cloud perception and 3D reconstruction workflow studied for an UR3e-based robotics project.

SIGMA Clermont2025–2026
01 / The challenge

The challenge

Reconstructing a shoe requires images from multiple viewpoints, consistent reference frames and processing of incomplete depth data.

02 / My work and methods

My work and methods

The work explored RGB-D acquisition, ROS 2 transforms and camera-topic publishing, point-cloud generation and Open3D TSDF reconstruction. Robot/camera integration with the UR3e was addressed in a simulation environment.

03 / Results and takeaways

Results and takeaways

The supplied files show reconstructed TUM dataset scenes and several 3D representations of a shoe. They document processing milestones, without establishing a fully validated end-to-end autonomous scanning system.
